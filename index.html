Operator prices for nearby search

RM per kWh used for chargers found by Google, matched by operator name. Your saved chargers always use their own price. Defaults: Gentari destination rate RM1.60 DC (highway sites are RM1.70, battery-backed RM1.80), Tesla owner rate RM1.02 DC, everyone else assumed to match Gentari.

Gentari credit deal

One top-up per session: you pay the first amount and get the second as charging credit, then stop. When a Gentari charger is in the comparison, all chargers are compared on adding that same energy.
